What's new in Actuent. Follow along with the RSS feed.

29 September 2026

Ask a site, similar sites, price history and LAWP 0.5

  • Ask a site: /api/ask answers a question from one site's own pages, with sources. On humans.actuent.ai every result has an "Ask" box.
  • Similar sites: /api/similar and the actuent_similar MCP tool find alternatives to any site.
  • "Ask this site" widget for site owners: one script tag adds a Lawpy button that answers visitors' questions from your pages.
  • Download lawp.json: every site page on Actuent now gives you a ready-to-publish LAWP file.
  • Price history: products include up to 90 days of prices and a "lowest in 90 days" flag.
  • Smarter searches: spelling fixes for whole searches ("accouting sofware"), and searches for places without a city favour your own city.
  • LAWP 0.5: standard action types (book_table, book_appointment, check_availability, request_quote and more).
  • Tools for the Vercel AI SDK and LangChain.js in @actuent/sdk 0.9, and ask/similar in the Python SDK 0.7.
  • Businesses in 50 more cities across the US, Australia, Asia, Latin America, Africa and the Middle East.
29 September 2026

Names first, answers from sites, and shopping in your market

  • Names first: search a company, product or place by name ("localilabs", "louvre tickets") and its own site comes first, followed by the company's other projects. Famous names use their official website.
  • Comparisons: "notion vs obsidian" puts both sites first and adds a comparison field.
  • Questions about a site: "does basecamp have a free plan" puts that site first, with an answer: sentences from its own pages and the page each came from.
  • Shopping in your market: products from shops in your country and currency come first, and shops you can't realistically buy from are left out. "shoes under 500 dkk in copenhagen" means Danish shops, and sites in the searched city's country rank higher.
  • Local needs: "vegan", "dog friendly", "open late", "brunch sunday" and "open now" use the place's features and opening hours. Closed and vacant places are never shown.
  • A redesigned search page at humans.actuent.ai, with products, events, answers and comparisons.
  • Faster when it's busy: fast=1 gives a quick first answer, and the best-known sites always come back even when the full search is slow.
  • City museums: "museo madrid" or "musée paris" puts the city's best-known museums first. Misspelt names ("spotfy") get a "did you mean".
  • Meet Lawpy, our mascot, on the search page and in the docs.
28 September 2026

Search that understands questions, typos and "near me"

  • Ask in plain words: "where can I buy running shoes in London?" and "coffee near me" just work, and misspellings get a "did you mean".
  • Better results: exact brand searches put the brand first, the page that answers the search comes first ("basecamp pricing"), results people open rank higher, and error pages, bot checks and parked domains are gone.
  • Every result has a snippet and a match score; responses can include related searches and upcoming events.
  • New search options: limit and offset, category, city, open_now, lang and sort.
  • Autocomplete: api.actuent.ai/api/autocomplete, used by the search page, which also has shareable links.
  • SDKs: @actuent/sdk 0.6.0 and actuent 0.4.0 handle busy times politely; the next versions add the new options.
28 September 2026

Clear messages when it's busy, and Pro first

  • Clear messages when it's busy: limited or empty results now say why in plain English (a message and notices), and busy responses ask you to try again shortly instead of failing silently. Errors & busy times.
  • Pro goes first: when lots of people are searching, Pro keeps the full search, live site visits and map tools; free searches get a simpler search from the index, with a notice.
  • MCP tools answer with a readable explanation instead of a bare error, and rate limits say how long to wait.
  • Better ranking: well-known sites are considered first, and a business's city, type and category are searchable ("barber amsterdam").
  • Faster, better index updates: sites are converted with a mix of rules and AI, popular sites first, and dead domains are removed from search.
27 September 2026

Search in your language, local businesses and tool examples

  • Search in your own language: common words in 11 European languages (and compounds like laufschuhe) are translated instantly, so passwort-manager finds the same sites as password manager.
  • Why this result: every search result has a matched note saying which words matched where.
  • Results that match every word of a search rank above partial matches.
  • Local businesses from OpenStreetMap: shops, restaurants, clinics and venues with a website are added city by city, with address, phone and opening hours.
  • What's on: venues' events pages are read twice a week.
  • More shops: every indexed site is checked for Shopify and WooCommerce products, not only the most popular ones; checkout links work for all Shopify products.
  • Cleaner page text: cookie banners, menus and copyright lines are removed.
  • Site pages: breadcrumbs, full schema.org business data, and a weekly State of the AI web post with an RSS feed.
  • Tool examples: a real request and response for every tool.
  • actuent_nearby understands everyday words ("coffee" finds cafés); events leave out betting spam.
  • Security and privacy: webhooks only go to public https addresses, trending shows only plain-word searches, and very long queries are refused.
27 September 2026

Faster search, checkout links and the Shopify app

  • Search is much faster: known sites answer in about a second instead of up to 25, and keyword searches overlap their steps.
  • Better results: sites are ranked with real popularity data, and adult and gambling sites are left out unless asked for. A weekly benchmark tracks quality.
  • Checkout links: Shopify products come with a cart_url, and actuent_cart puts several items in one checkout.
  • Actuent for Shopify: publishes a store's LAWP automatically (coming to the Shopify App Store).
  • npx @actuent/lawp: check, validate, create and test LAWP files from the command line.
  • Several API keys per account, with labels, revoking and a key history (Analytics → Account).
  • Free results only mention Pro when something was actually held back.
  • Search engines hear about new site pages right away (IndexNow).
  • Abuse protection: clients that keep hammering the API are blocked for an hour.
27 September 2026

Connected accounts and safer site claiming

  • Connect your account on a site (LAWP 0.4 user accounts) so your assistant can do things like reorder: sign in to the site once, and Actuent keeps the token encrypted and refreshes it. New tool: actuent_accounts.
  • Claiming a site now needs your personal verification code, as a homepage meta tag, a DNS record or a field in your lawp.json. Publishing a LAWP alone is no longer enough, so nobody else can claim your site.
  • WordPress plugin 1.3.1: paste your verification code in Settings → Actuent to claim your site.
27 September 2026

LAWP 0.4

  • Link your LAWP from any page (<link rel="lawp">, a Link header or robots.txt): Shopify, Squarespace, Wix and Webflow sites can publish their own now.
  • Safety labels: actions that cost money or can't be undone need the user's confirmation; Actuent enforces it.
  • Quotes (price and availability before committing), standard results and errors, and long-running actions with actuent_action_status.
  • Business details, hours and offers in LAWP itself; user accounts via OAuth 2.1; translations and split files for big sites.
  • Requests are signed with standard HTTP Message Signatures (RFC 9421, as in Web Bot Auth) as well as Actuent's original headers.
  • A JSON Schema and conformance tests for LAWP; the LAWP Checker validates against it.
  • WordPress plugin 1.3.0, Cloudflare Worker 1.2.0, @actuent/sdk 0.5.0 and Python 0.3.0 support 0.4.
  • The crawlers try harder before saving a site as minimal: the rendered page, www/http, llms.txt and the sitemap.

  • What's on pages and calendar feeds for every city: api.actuent.ai/site/in/<city>/whats-on.ics.
  • Site pages show which AI bots a site's robots.txt blocks, with the fix.
  • visit_url on every result: owners see the visits Actuent sends them in Analytics.
  • The crawlers try harder before saving a site as minimal: the rendered page, www and http addresses, llms.txt and the sitemap.
  • Python SDK 0.2 and @actuent/sdk 0.4 cover every tool; OpenAPI 1.1.

Plan an outing, compare products, badge widget and city pages

  • actuent_compare compares products too: price, stock, 90-day price range and cheaper shops.
  • actuent_watch_price can alert when a sold-out product is back in stock.
  • Pre-filled booking links: for Calendly, Cal.com, OpenTable, Resy and SevenRooms, the user's date, time, party size, name and email are filled in.
  • The badge grows up: a card style, badge.json and an embeddable widget, <script src="https://api.actuent.ai/badge.js" data-domain="yoursite.com">.
  • Site pages compare you with similar sites nearby and show what they have that you don't; the weekly score email does too.
  • Executable endpoints are tested daily; site pages and the LAWP Checker show 30-day reliability.
  • Platform guides for Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, static hosts and your own server.
  • Every site gets a category, and businesses are listed by city at api.actuent.ai/site/in/<city>.
  • Popular sites are refreshed by how often agents actually ask for them.
  • Parked domains and duplicates are flagged and left out of search.

Business details, open now, price changes and site pages

  • Search results include business details from a site's schema.org data: address, phone, opening hours and price range, plus open_now.

  • Every indexed site has a public page at api.actuent.ai/site/<domain> with its readiness score, actions and business details.
  • The badge at api.actuent.ai/badge.svg?domain= shows a live agent-readiness score out of 100.
  • Social preview images for Actuent pages.
  • Owners get a welcome email when they claim a site.
  • The crawler finds subpages from each site's sitemap and uses more free AI providers.
  • Security headers on every Actuent site, plus Dependabot and CodeQL on the public repositories.
  • WordPress plugin 1.1.0: the contact action asks for name, email and message.

Rule-based conversion and a faster backlog

  • New sites are paused until every existing minimal entry has been retried once.
