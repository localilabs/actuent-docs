Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 (rev. 5)

Actuent ("we", "us") is a search service for AI agents, made by localilabs (Alfred Murray) in Copenhagen, Denmark. This policy explains what data Actuent handles when you or your AI assistant use actuent.ai, api.actuent.ai, agents.actuent.ai (the Actuent MCP server, including the ChatGPT app and Claude connector), humans.actuent.ai, analytics.actuent.ai, docs.actuent.ai and the @actuent/sdk package. We are the data controller.

What we collect

  • Search requests. The query or tool arguments your AI assistant sends (for example "barber amsterdam" or a domain), the domains returned, the plan tier and a timestamp. We do not receive your conversation with the assistant, only what it sends to our tools.
  • IP address. Used to apply per-minute rate limits to free usage. It is stored for about 10 minutes and then deleted.
  • Account data (Pro). Your email address and API key. We store API keys only as one-way hashes in our logs and lookups. Search history made with a Pro key is linked to that key so we can provide the history and memory features and your analytics.
  • Connecting from ChatGPT or Claude (Pro). When you connect your Pro account, you paste your API key on our sign-in page. We give the app an encrypted access token linked to that key. We never see your ChatGPT or Claude password or conversations.
  • Actions (Pro). When your assistant performs an action on a site (for example sending a message or making a booking), we send the action and the input it provides to that site's own endpoint. We keep an action log with the site, the action, the first 500 characters of the input, whether it succeeded, and the first 1,000 characters of the site's reply, so you can see what your agents did. The site receives the input and handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Payment data (Pro). Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details.
  • Location (optional). If you use the nearby-places tool, the location and query your assistant provides are sent to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), or Google Maps Platform where configured, to find places. We do not store them beyond the search log.
  • News topics (optional). If you use the news tool, the topic is sent to Google News to find articles.
  • Referrals (Pro). If you share your referral link, we record clicks and which checkout sessions used your code, and apply a Stripe account credit to your email's Stripe customer. The person you referred sees nothing about you.
  • Site registrations. If you register a site via @actuent/sdk or the suggestion form, we store the site information you submit and which API key verified ownership of it.

How we use it

  • To answer searches and run the tools you call.
  • To prevent abuse through rate limits.
  • To provide Pro features: memory, search and action history, and analytics.
  • To show owners of verified sites how many actions agents ran on their site (counts only, never who or what was sent).
  • To show aggregate, non-personal statistics such as total searches and trending sites.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Website content

Actuent reads publicly available web pages and stores structured summaries of them (LAWP). This content is about websites, not about you. Our crawler respects robots.txt and identifies itself; see docs.actuent.ai/bot.

This includes business details that a site publishes as schema.org data (such as address, phone, opening hours and price range), and the public prices of products in online shops, including a history of price changes. Each indexed site has a public page at api.actuent.ai/site/<domain> showing this information.

For site owners

  • If you publish /.well-known/lawp.json (for example with our WordPress plugin), we read and index it like any public page.
  • Our WordPress plugin sends no data to us. It only downloads Actuent's public signing keys to check that incoming action requests really come from Actuent. Messages sent through its contact action go straight from your site to your admin email; Actuent's action log keeps only the short record described above.
  • When you claim a site with a Pro key, we send one welcome email to your account's email address (via Resend). We send no marketing email.
  • You can ask us to correct or remove your site's data, including its public site page, at any time.

Service providers

We use these providers to run Actuent. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Vercel (hosting)
  • Supabase (database)
  • Groq (AI processing of website content and search queries)
  • Jina AI (fetching public web pages)
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim or Google Maps Platform (nearby places, only when that tool is used)
  • Google News (news topics, only when the news tool is used)
  • Stripe (payments and referral credits)
  • Resend (sending the welcome email when you claim a site)
  • Cloudflare Workers AI, Cohere, Hugging Face, Google Gemini and Mistral (backup AI processing of public website content only)
  • European Central Bank exchange rates via frankfurter.app (currency conversion; no personal data)

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU/EEA under appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

  • Rate-limit records: about 10 minutes.
  • Search logs, action logs and Pro history: kept while needed to provide the service and analytics, and deleted on request.
  • Sign-in tokens from ChatGPT or Claude: access tokens last 1 hour and refresh tokens 90 days. They stop working immediately if your Pro plan is cancelled.
  • Account data: kept while your account is active, and deleted on request after cancellation.

Your rights

You can erase your search history, action log and webhooks yourself in Analytics → Account → Delete my data. Under the GDPR you can also ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and object to or restrict its processing. Contact us at support@localilabs.com. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).
