Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Actuent ("we", "us") is a search service for AI agents, run by Alfred Murray in Copenhagen, Denmark. This policy explains what data Actuent handles when you or your AI assistant use actuent.ai, api.actuent.ai, agents.actuent.ai (the Actuent MCP server, including the ChatGPT app and Claude connector), humans.actuent.ai, analytics.actuent.ai, docs.actuent.ai and the @actuent/sdk package. We are the data controller.

What we collect

  • Search requests. The query or tool arguments your AI assistant sends (for example "barber amsterdam" or a domain), the domains returned, the plan tier and a timestamp. We do not receive your conversation with the assistant, only what it sends to our tools.
  • IP address. Used to apply per-minute rate limits to free usage. It is stored for about 10 minutes and then deleted.
  • Account data (Pro). Your email address and API key. Search history made with a Pro key is linked to that key so we can provide the history and memory features and your analytics.
  • Payment data (Pro). Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details.
  • Location (optional). If you use the nearby-places tool, the location and query your assistant provides are sent to Google Maps Platform to find places. We do not store them beyond the search log.
  • Site registrations. If you register a site via @actuent/sdk or the suggestion form, we store the site information you submit.

How we use it

  • To answer searches and run the tools you call.
  • To prevent abuse through rate limits.
  • To provide Pro features: memory, search history and analytics.
  • To show aggregate, non-personal statistics such as total searches and trending sites.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Website content

Actuent reads publicly available web pages and stores structured summaries of them (LAWP). This content is about websites, not about you.

Service providers

We use these providers to run Actuent. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Vercel (hosting)
  • Supabase (database)
  • Groq (AI processing of website content and search queries)
  • Jina AI (fetching public web pages)
  • Google Maps Platform (nearby places, only when that tool is used)
  • Stripe (payments)

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU/EEA under appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

  • Rate-limit records: about 10 minutes.
  • Search logs and Pro search history: kept while needed to provide the service and analytics, and deleted on request.
  • Account data: kept while your account is active, and deleted on request after cancellation.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and object to or restrict its processing. Contact us at support@localilabs.com. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date at the top.
